CrowWatch — ESP32-4848S040

Flash the latest firmware straight to the board in your hand. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer.

1. Plug the 4" board into this computer's USB.

2. Click Install and pick the board's serial port.

3. Let it finish, then tap reset (or unplug/replug).

Your browser can't do web serial — use Chrome or Edge on desktop. Serial access was blocked. Reload and allow the port.

480×480 ST7701 panel, GT911 touch, ESP32-S3. Flashes a single image to 0x0. A fork of SquachWatch-CYD; source at DezusAZ/CrowWatch.
